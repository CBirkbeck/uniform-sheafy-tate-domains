WeightedParity.HeadModelData

Mathematical summary.

For an arbitrary weight w, this structure records a finite N, rational data in 𝒜_w⁽ᴺ⁾ defining the same rational subset as the original data in 𝒜_w, and a ring equivalence between the two completed localisations together with continuity in both directions.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

A finite-head model of a rational localization of 𝒜

Used in the paper for: Corollary 8.7 (finite-variable model and bicontinuous equivalence)

structure HeadModelData (D : RationalLocData (WPA K w)) : Type _ where
  /-- The head stage. -/
  N : ℕ
  /-- The head datum. -/
  DH : RationalLocData (WPHead K w N)
  /-- The head datum is rational. -/
  hDH : DH.IsRational
  /-- The lifted datum cuts the same rational subset as `D`. -/
  hopen : rationalOpen (liftDatum DH hDH).T (liftDatum DH hDH).s =
    rationalOpen D.T D.s
  /-- The model isomorphism. -/
  e : presheafValue D ≃+* TailC0 w N (QHead DH) (rhoQ DH)
  /-- Forward continuity. -/
  he : Continuous e
  /-- Backward continuity. -/
  he' : Continuous e.symm
