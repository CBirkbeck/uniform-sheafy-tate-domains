FiniteJet.GraphKoszul.isClosed_range_koszulDifferential

Mathematical summary.

For every q ≥ 0, im(d_{q+1,E}) is closed in ∧^qT_E^m; when q = 0, this says that I_E = (r₁,…,r_m) is closed in T_E.

Lean uses positive-degree exactness and continuity when q > 0, and the noetherian closed-ideal theorem when q = 0.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

Every Koszul image is closed ([FJP] Lemma 5.1 clause 2, closedness; clause 3 is
the q = 0 case). In positive degree im d_{n+1} = ker d_n is closed as the preimage of
{0} under the continuous differential d_n (koszulGraph_restricted_exact +
koszulDifferential_continuous + T2); the bottom image im d_0 = J_E is closed by
isClosed_graphIdeal, transported along the degree-0 conjugation
koszulDifferential_zero_eq_d1 and the set identity range_d1.

Used in the paper for: Lemma 5.1 (unit-ball specialization: closed image)

    IsClosed (Set.range (koszulDifferential r q))
