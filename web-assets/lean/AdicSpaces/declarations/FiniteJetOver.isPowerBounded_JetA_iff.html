FiniteJetOver.isPowerBounded_JetA_iff

Mathematical summary.

An element of 𝓐 is power-bounded if and only if its norm is at most one. Lean first proves ‖aⁿ‖=‖a‖ⁿ; norm at most one bounds the powers, while norm greater than one contradicts topological boundedness after testing against a small power of the uniformizer. Thus 𝓐° is the norm unit ball, which is the paper’s A₀.
