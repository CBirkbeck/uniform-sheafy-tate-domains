FiniteJet.IsFJPBase

Mathematical summary.

An IsFJPBase K is a complete ultrametric normed field together with a chosen element ϖ such that ϖ ≠ 0 and ‖ϖ‖ < 1. These are the only base-field hypotheses used by the finite-jet ring theory, the bad chart, and the Scottish Book Problem 24 and 28 arguments; no discreteness assumption is imposed.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

The base of the finite-jet construction: a complete ultrametric nonarchimedean
field together with a pseudouniformizer ϖ (ϖ ≠ 0, ‖ϖ‖ < 1). No discreteness is
required.

Used in the paper for: Theorem 1.1 (the general complete ultrametric base-field hypothesis); Proposition 7.1 and Appendix A (hypothesis on the abstract base field)

class IsFJPBase (K : Type u) [NormedField K] [IsUltrametricDist K] [CompleteSpace K] where
  /-- The chosen pseudouniformizer. -/
  pseudoUniformizer : K
  /-- The pseudouniformizer is nonzero (hence a unit, `K` being a field). -/
  pseudoUniformizer_ne_zero : pseudoUniformizer ≠ 0
  /-- The pseudouniformizer is topologically nilpotent. -/
  norm_pseudoUniformizer_lt_one : ‖pseudoUniformizer‖ < 1
