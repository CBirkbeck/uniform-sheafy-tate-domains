FiniteJet.Pinch.loc_pair_isEmbedding

Mathematical summary.

For the localized row of an abstract pinch square, the map A_α→B_α×C_α is a topological embedding. The proof derives a quotient-norm estimate from the bounded ideal pullback and then applies the resulting antilipschitz estimate.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

Topological strictness on the left ([FJP] (4.19)/(4.20)): A_α carries the
subspace topology of B_α × C_α.
