WeightedParity.wp_stronglySheafy

Mathematical summary.

For every weight w and every s≥0, the shifted-weight algebra WPA K (shiftWeight w s), which models adjoining s Tate variables to 𝒜_w, is sheafy for every ring of integral elements.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

Strong sheafiness ([WP] thm 6.2 (2): "(𝒜,𝒜°) is strongly sheafy"): for every
s, the weighted-parity model of the Tate extension 𝒜⟨V_1,…,V_s⟩ is sheafy for
every valid ring of integral elements. Combined with the identification
tateExtEquiv this is the statement in the project's own Tate-extension
vocabulary.
