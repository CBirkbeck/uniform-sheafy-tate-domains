FiniteJetOver.finiteJet_structurePresheaf_isSheafOfTopologicalRings_all_of_dvr

Mathematical summary.

If the valuation ring of K is a discrete valuation ring and A⁺ is any ring of integral elements of 𝓐, then the all-open structure presheaf on Spa(𝓐,A⁺) is a sheaf of topological rings.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.
