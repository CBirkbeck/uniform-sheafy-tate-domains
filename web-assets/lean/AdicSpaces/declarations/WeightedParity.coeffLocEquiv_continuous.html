WeightedParity.coeffLocEquiv_continuous

Mathematical summary.

The coefficientwise equivalence from the rational localisation of 𝒜 to the ring of coefficient families is continuous.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.
