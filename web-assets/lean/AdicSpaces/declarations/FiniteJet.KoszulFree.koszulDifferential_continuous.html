FiniteJet.KoszulFree.koszulDifferential_continuous

Mathematical summary.

Give ∧^q T_E^m the finite-product topology in the standard exterior basis. For every q ≥ 0, the Koszul differential d_{q+1,E}: ∧^{q+1}T_E^m → ∧^qT_E^m is continuous.

Lean proves this over any topological ring, since each coordinate is a finite sum of continuous multiplication maps.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

Over a topological ring, the Koszul differential is continuous for the product
topologies: each coordinate is a finite sum of continuous coordinate multiplications.
