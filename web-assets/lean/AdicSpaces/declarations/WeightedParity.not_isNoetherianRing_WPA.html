WeightedParity.not_isNoetherianRing_WPA

Mathematical summary.

Let w:ℕ→ℕ satisfy w(n)≥1 for every n≥1. Then 𝒜_w=WPA K w is not noetherian. Coefficient extraction kills U_1²,…,U_m² but not U_{m+1}², so the ideal generated by the elements U_n² is not finitely generated.
