FiniteJetOver.finiteJet_isSheafyFor

Mathematical summary.

Choose a uniformizer of K and suppose that the unit ball of K is noetherian. Then the all-open structure presheaf on Spa(𝓐,𝓐°) is a sheaf of topological rings.
