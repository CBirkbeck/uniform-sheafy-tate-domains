FiniteJet.scottishWitness_mul_isometry

Mathematical summary.

Let f=Q² in the finite-jet algebra over a base satisfying IsFJPBase K. Multiplication by f preserves distances, because the multiplicative Gauss norm gives ‖Q²a‖=‖a‖. It is consequently injective and identifies the algebra homeomorphically with its range. Since the source is complete, that range is closed. These are the strictness and closed-image assertions required in Problem 28.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

Multiplication by Q² is an isometry of 𝓐. This single fact gives injectivity,
strictness and closedness of the image at once, with no appeal to a nonarchimedean open
mapping theorem.
