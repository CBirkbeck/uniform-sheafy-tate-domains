WeightedParity.weightedParity_chart_isDomain

Mathematical summary.

The section ring on {|W| ≤ |ϖ|} is an integral domain. Lean identifies its head quotient with K⟨X⟩, observes that the twist ϖX is regular, embeds the resulting c₀ tail algebra into formal multivariable power series, and transports the domain property through the coefficientwise localization equivalence.
