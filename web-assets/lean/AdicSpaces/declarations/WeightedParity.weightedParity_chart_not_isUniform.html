WeightedParity.weightedParity_chart_not_isUniform

Mathematical summary.

The section ring on {|W| ≤ |ϖ|} is not uniform. Lean constructs power-bounded elements T_n with ‖T_n²‖=1 but ‖T_n‖=|ϖ|^{-n}; their norms are unbounded. The contradiction is transported through the bicontinuous coefficientwise localization equivalence.
