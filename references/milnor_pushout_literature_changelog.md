# Pushout and Milnor-square revision

- Replaced the earlier uncertainty about the finite-jet square by the precise categorical result:
  - it is a pushout in the full subcategory of affinoid adic spaces;
  - it is not a pushout in the category of all adic spaces.
- Added the affinoid proof using
  \(A^\circ=B^\circ\times_{D^\circ}C^\circ\) and Huber's affinoid mapping theorem.
- Added an explicit nonaffinoid target showing failure of uniqueness in the global pushout universal property:
  - both comparison spaces factor through a proper open \(U\subsetneq \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^\circ)\);
  - two copies of \(\operatorname{Spa}(A,A^\circ)\) glued along \(U\) give two distinct maps agreeing on both comparison spaces.
- Added the general doubling obstruction: a cocone whose legs factor through a proper open of its vertex cannot be a pushout in the category of adic spaces.
- Clarified that the possible existence of a different, necessarily nonaffinoid, pushout object remains open for this span.
- Expanded the literature discussion:
  - explained that the name “Milnor square” comes from Milnor's projective-module patching theorem;
  - credited Kerz--Saito--Tamme for explicit use of Milnor squares of complete Tate rings, compatible rings of definition, the open-mapping argument, and analytic K-theory excision;
  - stated that the exact phrase “strict Milnor square” was not found as established terminology in this nonarchimedean setting;
  - isolated the new contribution as preservation under completed rational localization together with sheaf transfer.
