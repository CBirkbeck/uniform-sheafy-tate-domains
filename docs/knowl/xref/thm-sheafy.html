Corollary 6.2.

For every \(s\geq0\) and every ring of integral elements
\begin{equation*} A_s^+\subseteq A\langle V_1,\ldots,V_s\rangle^\circ, \end{equation*}
the Huber pair
\begin{equation*} \bigl(A\langle V_1,\ldots,V_s\rangle,A_s^+\bigr) \end{equation*}
is sheafy. In particular, \(A\) is strongly sheafy.
