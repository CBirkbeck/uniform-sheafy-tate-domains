Lemma 7.2. Isometric finite-stage normal form.

There is an isometric decomposition
\begin{equation} \mathcal A_N\cong \bigoplus_{\epsilon\in\{0,1\}^N}^{\max} k\langle W,Z_1,\ldots,Z_N\rangle Y^\epsilon.\tag{7.8} \end{equation}
Under the substitution
\begin{equation} Y_n\longmapsto W^nU_n, \qquad Z_n\longmapsto U_n^2,\tag{7.9} \end{equation}
this identifies \(\mathcal A_N\) isometrically with the closed subalgebra of \(k\langle W,U_1,\ldots,U_N\rangle\) supported on the monomials in (7.2) involving only \(U_1,\ldots,U_N\text{.}\) The transition maps \(\mathcal A_N\to\mathcal A_{N+1}\) are isometric, and
\begin{equation} \mathcal A=\widehat{\bigcup_{N\geq1}\mathcal A_N}.\tag{7.10} \end{equation}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Writing \(\nu_n=2q_n+\epsilon_n\) gives the unique factorization
\begin{equation} W^aU^\nu =W^{a-\omega(\nu)} \prod_{n=1}^NY_n^{\epsilon_n}Z_n^{q_n}\tag{7.11} \end{equation}
whenever \(a\geq\omega(\nu)\) and \(\nu\) is supported in \(\{1,\ldots,N\}\text{.}\) Thus the finite-head support algebra is the sum of the subspaces
\begin{equation*} k\langle W,Z_1,\ldots,Z_N\rangle Y^\epsilon, \qquad \epsilon\in\{0,1\}^N. \end{equation*}
The summands have disjoint monomial supports, distinguished by the parities of the \(U_n\)-exponents. The sum is therefore direct and its Gauss norm is the maximum of the norms of its components.
🔗
The substitution (7.9) kills \(Y_n^2-W^{2n}Z_n\) and hence gives a norm-nonincreasing map from the quotient (7.7) to this support algebra. In the other direction, send a parity decomposition \(\sum_\epsilon f_\epsilon Y^\epsilon\) to the class represented by the same sum. This map is also norm-nonincreasing, and (7.11) shows that the two maps are inverse. This proves the isometric normal form. The same support description makes the transition maps isometric. Finally, every finite set of allowed monomials lies in one head, and finite-support series are dense in (7.5); hence (7.10) follows.
