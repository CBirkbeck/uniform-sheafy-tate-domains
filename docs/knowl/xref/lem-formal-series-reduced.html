Lemma 7.11.

Let \(P\) be a reduced ring and let \(J\) be any index set. Define
\begin{equation} \mathcal F_J(P)= \prod_{\mu\in\mathbf N^{(J)}}P\,U^\mu,\tag{7.24} \end{equation}
with multiplication by coefficientwise finite convolution. Then \(\mathcal F_J(P)\) is reduced.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Since \(P\) is reduced, the diagonal map
\begin{equation*} P\longrightarrow \prod_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(P)}P/\mathfrak p \end{equation*}
is injective. Applying formal power series coefficientwise, and then commuting formal power series with this product, gives an injection
\begin{equation*} \mathcal F_J(P)\longrightarrow \prod_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(P)} \mathcal F_J(P/\mathfrak p). \end{equation*}
Each factor on the right is a domain, since its coefficient ring is a domain. The product is therefore reduced, and so is \(\mathcal F_J(P)\text{.}\)
