Lemma 7.11.

Let \(P\) be a reduced ring and let \(J\) be any index set. Define
\begin{equation} \mathcal F_J(P)= \prod_{\mu\in\mathbf N^{(J)}}P\,U^\mu,\tag{7.34} \end{equation}
with multiplication by coefficientwise finite convolution. Then \(\mathcal F_J(P)\) is reduced.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For every finite subset \(J_0\subset J\text{,}\) setting the variables outside \(J_0\) equal to zero gives a homomorphism
\begin{equation*} \tau_{J_0}:\mathcal F_J(P)\longrightarrow P[[U_j:j\in J_0]]. \end{equation*}
These homomorphisms jointly detect all coefficients. A finite-variable formal power-series ring over a reduced ring is reduced: the injection \(P\to\prod_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(P)}P/\mathfrak p\) induces an injection of formal power-series rings into a product of domains. If an element of \(\mathcal F_J(P)\) is nilpotent, every \(\tau_{J_0}\) kills it; joint coefficient detection then kills the original element.
