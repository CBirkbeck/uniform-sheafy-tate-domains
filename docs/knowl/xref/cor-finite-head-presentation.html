Corollary 7.10. Finite-head presentation.

Every rational localization of \(\mathcal A\) is canonically isomorphic to a ring of the form
\begin{equation} \widehat{\bigoplus}_{\mu}^{\,c_0}Pe_\mu,\tag{7.28} \end{equation}
where \(P\) is a rational localization of some affinoid head \(\mathcal A_N\text{.}\) Under this identification the inclusions obtained by enlarging the head have norm-nonincreasing algebra retractions, and their union is dense.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Start with a rational datum in \(\mathcal A\text{.}\) Multiplying all entries by one power of \(\varpi\) does not change the rational subset or localization, so assume that the entries lie in \(\mathcal A_0\text{.}\) Choose an integral Bezout relation
\begin{equation*} \varpi^\ell=\sum_j a_jd_j, \qquad a_j\in\mathcal A_0. \end{equation*}
By (7.10), for some \(N\) the entries \(d_j\) admit approximations \(d_j'\in\mathcal A_N\cap\mathcal A_0\) satisfying \(d_j'-d_j\in\varpi^{\ell+1}\mathcal A_0\text{.}\) By Lemma 7.8, the primed datum gives the same rational subset and the same completed localization. The primed integral Bezout relation produced in that lemma remains valid after applying the retraction \(\rho_N\text{;}\) hence the primed datum is a rational datum already in \(\mathcal A_N\text{.}\) Now apply Proposition 7.9.
🔗
For \(M\geq N\text{,}\) put \(P_M=(\mathcal A_M)_\alpha\text{.}\) The same proposition, with head \(M\text{,}\) identifies the localization with the \(c_0\)-sum over the variables of index \(>M\text{.}\) Coefficient projection gives a norm-nonincreasing retraction to \(P_M\text{,}\) and finite tail truncation proves that \(\bigcup_MP_M\) is dense.
