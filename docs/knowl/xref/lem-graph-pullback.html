Lemma 5.2.

The graph ideal \(J_A\) is closed in \(P_A\text{,}\) and the canonical map
\begin{equation*} J_A\longrightarrow J_B\times_{J_D}J_C \end{equation*}
is an isomorphism. Moreover,
\begin{equation} 0\longrightarrow J_A\longrightarrow J_B\oplus J_C \longrightarrow J_D\longrightarrow 0\tag{5.4} \end{equation}
is strict exact.

Proof.
We first prove the algebraic pullback statement. Let \((x_B,x_C)\in J_B\times_{J_D}J_C\text{.}\) Choose \(u_B\in P_B^m\) and \(u_C\in P_C^m\) with \(d_{1,B}(u_B)=x_B\) and \(d_{1,C}(u_C)=x_C\text{.}\) Their difference in \(P_D^m\) lies in \(\ker(d_{1,D})\text{.}\) By Lemma 5.1, it is \(d_{2,D}(v_D)\) for some \(v_D\in\bigwedge^2P_D^m\text{.}\) Lift \(v_D\) to \(v_C\in\bigwedge^2P_C^m\) and replace \(u_C\) by \(u_C+d_{2,C}(v_C)\text{.}\) The new pair of coefficient vectors has the same image in \(P_D^m\text{,}\) hence comes from \(u_A\in P_A^m\) by (5.3). Then \(d_{1,A}(u_A)\) maps to \((x_B,x_C)\text{.}\) This proves the asserted algebraic isomorphism.
🔗
We next verify bounded denominators. Choose exponents \(h_B,h_C,z_D\) as in Lemma 5.1, and put \(h=\max\{h_B,h_C\}\text{.}\) Suppose that \((x_B,x_C)\) belongs to the pullback and lies in \(P_{B,0}\oplus P_{C,0}\text{.}\) Choose \(u_B\in P_{B,0}^m\) and \(u_C\in P_{C,0}^m\) such that
\begin{equation*} d_{1,B}(u_B)=\varpi^h x_B, \qquad d_{1,C}(u_C)=\varpi^h x_C. \end{equation*}
The difference \(s=(u_B)_D-(u_C)_D\) belongs to \(\ker(d_{1,D})\cap P_{D,0}^m\text{.}\) By (5.2), choose \(v_D\in\bigwedge^2P_{D,0}^m\) with \(d_{2,D}(v_D)=\varpi^{z_D}s\text{.}\) Lift \(v_D\) to \(v_C\in\bigwedge^2P_{C,0}^m\) and set
\begin{equation*} u_C'=\varpi^{z_D}u_C+d_{2,C}(v_C). \end{equation*}
Then \((u_C')_D=\varpi^{z_D}(u_B)_D\) and \(d_{1,C}(u_C')=\varpi^{h+z_D}x_C\text{.}\) By (5.3), the compatible integral pair \((\varpi^{z_D}u_B,u_C')\) comes from \(u_A\in P_{A,0}^m\text{.}\) Thus
\begin{equation} \varpi^{h+z_D}\bigl((J_B\times_{J_D}J_C) \cap(P_{B,0}\oplus P_{C,0})\bigr) \subset d_{1,A}(P_{A,0}^m).\tag{5.5} \end{equation}
This proves strictness of the pullback identification. The fiber product \(J_B\times_{J_D}J_C\) is closed in \(P_B\oplus P_C\text{,}\) because \(J_B\) and \(J_C\) are closed and the matching condition is closed. The integral row (5.3) identifies \(P_A\) strictly with the kernel of \(P_B\oplus P_C\to P_D\text{.}\) Together with (5.5), this shows that \(J_A\) is closed in \(P_A\text{.}\)
🔗
Finally, the map \(J_B\oplus J_C\to J_D\) is surjective: lift a coefficient vector in \(P_D^m\) to \(P_C^m\) and apply \(d_1\text{.}\) If the element of \(J_D\) is integral, then (5.1) for \(E=D\) shows that a fixed power of \(\varpi\) has an integral coefficient vector, which can be lifted to \(P_{C,0}^m\text{.}\) Thus this surjection is strict. Together with (5.5), this proves strict exactness of (5.4).
