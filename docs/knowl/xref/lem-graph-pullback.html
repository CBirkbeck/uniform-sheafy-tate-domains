Lemma 5.2.

The ideal \(I_A\) is closed in \(T_A\text{,}\) and the canonical map
\begin{equation*} I_A\longrightarrow I_B\times_{I_D}I_C \end{equation*}
is an isomorphism. Moreover,
\begin{equation} 0\longrightarrow I_A\longrightarrow I_B\oplus I_C \longrightarrow I_D\longrightarrow 0\tag{5.4} \end{equation}
is strict exact.

Proof.
We use the controlled lifts supplied in the proof of Lemma 5.1. Let \((x_B,x_C)\in I_B\times_{I_D}I_C\text{,}\) and choose lifts \(u_B\in T_B^m\) and \(u_C\in T_C^m\) of \(x_B\) and \(x_C\) whose norms are bounded by fixed multiples of \(\lVert x_B\rVert\) and \(\lVert x_C\rVert\text{.}\) In \(T_D^m\) the difference
\begin{equation*} w=(u_B)_D-(u_C)_D \end{equation*}
belongs to \(\ker(d_{1,D})\text{.}\) Choose a controlled lift \(v_D\in\bigwedge^2T_D^m\) with \(d_{2,D}(v_D)=w\text{,}\) and lift \(v_D\) coefficientwise to \(v_C\in\bigwedge^2T_C^m\text{.}\) The coefficientwise section \(T_C\to T_D\) preserves the norm. Replacing \(u_C\) by
\begin{equation*} u_C'=u_C+d_{2,C}(v_C) \end{equation*}
does not change \(d_{1,C}(u_C)\text{,}\) and the images of \(u_B\) and \(u_C'\) in \(T_D^m\) now agree. Exactness of (5.3), applied coefficientwise, gives \(u_A\in T_A^m\) mapping to this pair. Then \(d_{1,A}(u_A)\) maps to \((x_B,x_C)\text{.}\)
🔗
The same construction keeps track of the norms. The controlled lifts for \(d_{1,B}\text{,}\) \(d_{1,C}\) and \(d_{2,D}\text{,}\) continuity of \(d_{2,C}\text{,}\) and the norm-one coefficientwise section give a constant \(C\text{,}\) independent of \((x_B,x_C)\text{,}\) for which the lift \(x_A=d_{1,A}(u_A)\) may be chosen with
\begin{equation*} \lVert x_A\rVert\leq C\max\{\lVert x_B\rVert,\lVert x_C\rVert\}. \end{equation*}
The map \(T_A\to T_B\oplus T_C\) is injective, so this proves both the algebraic pullback assertion and the boundedness of its inverse.
🔗
It remains to see that \(I_A\) is closed. The ideals \(I_B\) and \(I_C\) are closed by Lemma 5.1. If \(x\) lies in the closure of \(I_A\text{,}\) its images lie in \(I_B\) and \(I_C\text{.}\) The controlled pullback just proved gives \(x_A\in I_A\) with the same two images as \(x\text{.}\) Injectivity of \(T_A\to T_B\oplus T_C\) gives \(x=x_A\text{,}\) as required.
🔗
Finally, let \(y\in I_D\text{.}\) A controlled lift of \(y\) through \(d_{1,D}\text{,}\) followed by the coefficientwise section \(T_D^m\to T_C^m\text{,}\) produces \(x_C\in I_C\) mapping to \(y\text{,}\) with norm bounded by a fixed multiple of \(\lVert y\rVert\text{.}\) Thus \(I_B\oplus I_C\to I_D\) is a strict surjection. Its kernel is \(I_A\) by the pullback assertion, and the preceding norm estimate makes the inclusion on the left strict. This proves (5.4).
