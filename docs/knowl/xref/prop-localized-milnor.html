Proposition 5.3.

For every rational datum \(\alpha\) in \(A\text{,}\) the canonical sequence
\begin{equation} 0\longrightarrow A_\alpha\longrightarrow B_\alpha\oplus C_\alpha\longrightarrow D_\alpha\longrightarrow 0\tag{5.6} \end{equation}
is strict exact, and \(C_\alpha\to D_\alpha\) is a strict surjection. Equivalently,
\begin{equation*} A_\alpha\cong B_\alpha\times_{D_\alpha}C_\alpha. \end{equation*}
The coefficient maps used in these identifications commute with rational restriction.

Proof.
By Lemma 5.2, all four ideals are closed. Hence (2.3) gives
\begin{equation*} E_\alpha=T_E/I_E \qquad(E=A,B,C,D). \end{equation*}
The two exact rows (5.3) and (5.4) give algebraic exactness of (5.6).
🔗
Let \(E_{\alpha,0}\) be the image of \(T_{E,0}\) in \(E_\alpha\text{;}\) this is a ring of definition. Since \(T_{C,0}\to T_{D,0}\) is surjective, so is \(C_{\alpha,0}\to D_{\alpha,0}\text{.}\) Thus \(C_\alpha\to D_\alpha\) is a strict surjection.
🔗
It remains to check strictness at the left. Let \(x\in A_\alpha\) have images in \(B_{\alpha,0}\) and \(C_{\alpha,0}\text{.}\) Choose representatives \(b_0\in T_{B,0}\) and \(c_0\in T_{C,0}\text{.}\) Their difference
\begin{equation*} \delta=(b_0)_D-(c_0)_D \end{equation*}
belongs to \(I_D\cap T_{D,0}\text{.}\) Choose \(h_D\) as in (5.1). There is \(u_D\in T_{D,0}^m\) with \(d_{1,D}(u_D)=\varpi^{h_D}\delta\text{.}\) Lift \(u_D\) to \(u_C\in T_{C,0}^m\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} \left(\varpi^{h_D}b_0, \varpi^{h_D}c_0+d_{1,C}(u_C)\right) \end{equation*}
is a compatible pair in \(T_{B,0}\oplus T_{C,0}\text{,}\) so it comes from \(T_{A,0}\text{.}\) Its class in \(A_\alpha\) is \(\varpi^{h_D}x\text{.}\) Therefore
\begin{equation*} \varpi^{h_D}\Bigl(A_\alpha\cap (B_{\alpha,0}\oplus C_{\alpha,0})\Bigr) \subset A_{\alpha,0}, \end{equation*}
which is the bounded-denominator criterion for strictness.
🔗
All maps used above come from the coefficient maps \(A\to B,C\) and \(B,C\to D\text{.}\) More generally, a homomorphism \(E\to E'\) carrying a rational datum \(\alpha\) to \(\alpha'\) induces a coefficientwise map \(T_E\to T_{E'}\) which sends the relations for \(\alpha\) to those for \(\alpha'\text{.}\) The pullback identifications therefore commute with these maps. Applying this to rational restriction, and then using presentation independence and transitivity, proves the asserted compatibility under refinement.
