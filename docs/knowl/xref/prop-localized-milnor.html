Proposition 5.3.

For every rational datum \(\alpha\) in \(A\text{,}\) the canonical sequence
\begin{equation} 0\longrightarrow A_\alpha\longrightarrow B_\alpha\oplus C_\alpha\longrightarrow D_\alpha\longrightarrow 0\tag{5.6} \end{equation}
is strict exact, and \(C_\alpha\to D_\alpha\) is a strict surjection. Equivalently,
\begin{equation*} A_\alpha\cong B_\alpha\times_{D_\alpha}C_\alpha. \end{equation*}
These identifications are natural under rational refinement.

Proof.
By Lemma 5.2, all four graph ideals are closed. Hence the graph model (2.3) gives
\begin{equation*} E_\alpha=P_E/J_E \qquad(E=A,B,C,D). \end{equation*}
The two exact rows (5.3) and (5.4) give algebraic exactness of (5.6).
🔗
Let \(E_{\alpha,0}\) be the image of \(P_{E,0}\) in \(E_\alpha\text{;}\) this is a ring of definition. Since \(P_{C,0}\to P_{D,0}\) is surjective, so is \(C_{\alpha,0}\to D_{\alpha,0}\text{.}\) Thus \(C_\alpha\to D_\alpha\) is a strict surjection.
🔗
It remains to check strictness at the left. Let \(x\in A_\alpha\) have images in \(B_{\alpha,0}\) and \(C_{\alpha,0}\text{.}\) Choose representatives \(b_0\in P_{B,0}\) and \(c_0\in P_{C,0}\text{.}\) Their difference
\begin{equation*} \delta=(b_0)_D-(c_0)_D \end{equation*}
belongs to \(J_D\cap P_{D,0}\text{.}\) Choose \(h_D\) as in (5.1). There is \(u_D\in P_{D,0}^m\) with \(d_{1,D}(u_D)=\varpi^{h_D}\delta\text{.}\) Lift \(u_D\) to \(u_C\in P_{C,0}^m\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} \left(\varpi^{h_D}b_0, \varpi^{h_D}c_0+d_{1,C}(u_C)\right) \end{equation*}
is a compatible pair in \(P_{B,0}\oplus P_{C,0}\text{,}\) so it comes from \(P_{A,0}\text{.}\) Its class in \(A_\alpha\) is \(\varpi^{h_D}x\text{.}\) Therefore
\begin{equation*} \varpi^{h_D}\Bigl(A_\alpha\cap (B_{\alpha,0}\oplus C_{\alpha,0})\Bigr) \subset A_{\alpha,0}, \end{equation*}
which is the bounded-denominator criterion for strictness.
🔗
All maps used above are induced by the original coefficient maps and the universal property of rational localization. Huber’s presentation independence and transitivity therefore make the pullback identifications natural under rational refinement.
