Proposition 4.1.

There is a canonical isomorphism of complete Tate \(k\)-algebras
\begin{equation*} A_{\mathrm{in}}\cong k\langle X,Q\rangle/(Q^2), \qquad W\longmapsto\varpi X. \end{equation*}

Proof.
Let
\begin{equation*} q\colon A_0[X]\longrightarrow A[X]/(\varpi X-W) \end{equation*}
be the composite of the inclusion \(A_0[X]\hookrightarrow A[X]\) with the quotient map, and set
\begin{equation*} G_0:=q(A_0[X]). \end{equation*}
Since \(\varpi\) is a unit in \(A\text{,}\) evaluation at \(X=W/\varpi\) gives a canonical isomorphism
\begin{equation*} A[X]/(\varpi X-W)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }A. \end{equation*}
Under this identification,
\begin{equation*} G_0=A_0[W/\varpi]\subset A; \end{equation*}
it is the ring of definition whose separated \(\varpi\)-adic completion defines \(A_{\mathrm{in}}\text{.}\) For \(y\in Q^2C_0\) and every \(n\geq 0\text{,}\) the support condition gives \(W^{-n}y\in A_0\text{.}\) In \(G_0\) one has
\begin{equation} y=W^n(W^{-n}y)=\varpi^nX^n(W^{-n}y)\in\varpi^nG_0.\tag{4.1} \end{equation}
Thus \(Q^2C_0\) maps to zero in the separated completion. After multiplying by a suitable power of \(\varpi\text{,}\) the same conclusion holds for every element of \(Q^2C\text{.}\)
🔗
Put
\begin{equation*} T_0=k^\circ\langle X,Q\rangle/(Q^2), \qquad T=T_0[1/\varpi]. \end{equation*}
Using the unique expression in (3.2), define
\begin{equation*} f_0(W)+Qf_1(W)+Q^2h \longmapsto f_0(\varpi X)+Qf_1(\varpi X). \end{equation*}
This sends \(A_0\) into \(T_0\text{,}\) and it sends \(W/\varpi\) to \(X\text{.}\) Hence it extends to a continuous homomorphism \(A_{\mathrm{in}}\to T\text{.}\)
🔗
Conversely, \(X\) and \(Q\) belong to the ring of definition of \(A_{\mathrm{in}}\text{,}\) and (4.1) gives \(Q^2=0\) there. They therefore define a continuous homomorphism \(T\to A_{\mathrm{in}}\text{.}\) The two composites fix \(X\) and \(Q\text{.}\) On the dense image of \(G_0\text{,}\) the second composite differs from the identity only by an element of \(Q^2C_0\text{,}\) which is zero by (4.1). The two maps are therefore inverse.
