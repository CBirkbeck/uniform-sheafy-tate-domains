Theorem 6.1. Milnor descent.

Let \(R,B,C,D\) be complete nonarchimedean Huber rings in a commutative square
\begin{equation*} \begin{matrix} R&\longrightarrow&C\\ \big\downarrow&&\big\downarrow\\ B&\longrightarrow&D. \end{matrix} \end{equation*}
Assume that rational data, covers, and matching families can be transported around the square, compatibly with restriction and passage to common refinements. Suppose also that, for every rational datum on \(R\text{,}\) the equaliser and topological-embedding conclusions of Proposition 5.3 hold for the induced data. If \(B,C,D\) are sheafy, then \(R\) is sheafy.

Proof.
Let \(U=\bigcup_iU_i\) be a finite rational cover. A matching family glues over \(B\) and \(C\text{.}\) The two resulting sections have the same image over \(D\text{,}\) so the equaliser property gives a unique section over \(R\text{.}\) The topological-embedding property, together with the corresponding property for \(B\) and \(C\text{,}\) gives the topological sheaf condition. The rational-basis theorem now gives the sheaf condition on all opens [bib-Stacks, Tag 009O].
