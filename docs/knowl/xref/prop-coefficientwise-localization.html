Proposition 7.9. Coefficientwise localization.

Let \(\alpha\) be a rational datum in \(\mathcal A_N\text{,}\) and put \(P=(\mathcal A_N)_\alpha\text{.}\) There is a canonical topological algebra isomorphism
\begin{equation} \mathcal A_\alpha \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } \widehat{\bigoplus}_{\mu}^{\,c_0}Pe_\mu.\tag{7.25} \end{equation}
It is natural for rational refinements represented in a finite head: if \(P\to Q\) is the corresponding head restriction map, the induced restriction sends
\begin{equation} \sum_\mu p_\mu e_\mu \longmapsto \sum_\mu (p_\mu|_Q)e_\mu.\tag{7.26} \end{equation}
The same statement holds after increasing \(N\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let
\begin{equation*} R=\mathcal A_N\langle T_1,\ldots,T_m\rangle, \qquad I=(gT_i-f_i)_i\subset R. \end{equation*}
Since \(\mathcal A_N\) is affinoid, the graph ideal \(I\) is closed and the graph map \(d_1:R^m\to I\) is strict; this is the degree-zero conclusion of Lemma 5.1. In particular, there is a constant \(C\) such that every \(y\in I\) has a lift \(x\in R^m\) with \(\lVert x\rVert\leq C\lVert y\rVert\text{.}\)
🔗
By (7.22),
\begin{equation*} \mathcal A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_m\rangle \cong \widehat{\bigoplus}_{\mu}^{\,c_0}Re_\mu. \end{equation*}
Because the graph relations lie in the finite head, the graph differential acts coefficientwise. Its image is exactly
\begin{equation*} \widehat{\bigoplus}_{\mu}^{\,c_0}Ie_\mu. \end{equation*}
Indeed, the inclusion from left to right is immediate. Conversely, for a null family \((y_\mu)_\mu\) in \(I\text{,}\) choose coefficientwise lifts \(x_\mu\) with \(\lVert x_\mu\rVert\leq C\lVert y_\mu\rVert\text{;}\) then \((x_\mu)_\mu\) is again a null family. Thus the graph ideal over \(\mathcal A\) is closed and
\begin{equation} \frac{\widehat{\bigoplus}_{\mu}^{c_0}Re_\mu} {\widehat{\bigoplus}_{\mu}^{c_0}Ie_\mu} \cong \widehat{\bigoplus}_{\mu}^{c_0}(R/I)e_\mu\tag{7.27} \end{equation}
as complete topological modules and as algebras. Since \(R/I=P\text{,}\) this proves (7.25).
🔗
Every map used in (7.27) is induced by the canonical graph quotient. If a further rational datum lies in the head, its graph differential is again coefficientwise, so the same construction gives (7.26). Presentation independence and transitivity of rational localization extend this compatibility to iterated refinements and to a larger finite head.
