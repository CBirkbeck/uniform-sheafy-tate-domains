Lemma 7.7. Small perturbation lemma.

Let \((E,E^+)\) be a complete Tate Huber pair and let \(\alpha=(f_1,\ldots,f_m;g)\) be a rational datum in \(E\text{.}\) There is a neighbourhood \(V\) of zero in \(E\) such that, whenever
\begin{equation*} g'-g,\ f_1'-f_1,\ldots,f_m'-f_m\in V, \end{equation*}
the tuple \(\alpha'=(f_1',\ldots,f_m';g')\) is a rational datum and \(\alpha\) and \(\alpha'\) define the same rational subset of \(\Spa(E,E^+)\text{.}\) Their completed rational localisations are canonically bicontinuously isomorphic over \(E\text{.}\)

Proof.
Apply [bib-Huber93, Lemma 3.10] after including \(g\) among the numerators; see also [bib-KL, Remark 2.4.7]. The assertion about completed rational localisations follows from their universal property [bib-Huber94, Proposition 1.3].
