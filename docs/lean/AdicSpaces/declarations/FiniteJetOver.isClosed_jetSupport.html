FiniteJetOver.isClosed_jetSupport

Mathematical summary.

The support condition defining the finite-jet algebra 𝓐 cuts out a closed subring of the complete ring 𝓒: membership of the constant and linear Q-coefficients in K⟨W⟩ is a closed condition. The immediately following instance therefore makes 𝓐 complete.
