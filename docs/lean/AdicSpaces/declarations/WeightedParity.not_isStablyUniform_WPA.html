WeightedParity.not_isStablyUniform_WPA

Mathematical summary.

If the weight w is unbounded on the positive integers, then 𝒜_w=WPA K w is not stably uniform, because its rational chart {|W|≤|ϖ|} is not uniform.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.
