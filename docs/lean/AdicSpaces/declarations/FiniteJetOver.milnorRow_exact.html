FiniteJetOver.milnorRow_exact

Mathematical summary.

For every pair (b,c) ∈ 𝓑 × 𝓒 with the same image in 𝓓, there is a unique a ∈ 𝓐 mapping to (b,c). Hence 𝓐 ≅ 𝓑 ×_𝓓 𝓒.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

Exactness in the middle with uniqueness: a compatible pair (b, c) comes from a unique
element of 𝓐 ([FJP] Prop 2.1: "Its kernel is the algebraic pullback (1.6)").

Used in the paper for: Appendix: exactness of the Milnor row

theorem milnorRow_exact (b : JetB K) (c : JetC K) (h : rhoB K b = rhoC K c) :
    ∃! a : JetA K, jB K a = b ∧ iotaC K a = c
