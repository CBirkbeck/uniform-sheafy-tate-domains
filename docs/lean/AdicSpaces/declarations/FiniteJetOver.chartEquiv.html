FiniteJetOver.chartEquiv

Mathematical summary.

There is a ring equivalence between the rational chart 𝒪_𝓐({|W| ≤ |ϖ|}) and K⟨X,Q⟩/(Q²). The forward map takes the first two Q-coefficients and rescales W to ϖX; the completed chart kills every element whose first two Q-coefficients vanish. The inverse evaluates K⟨X⟩ at W/ϖ and adjoins the square-zero image of Q. The inverse identities use the two-jet decomposition and polynomial density. The companion continuity theorems make this a homeomorphism.
