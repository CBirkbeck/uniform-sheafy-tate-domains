FiniteJetOver.chartEquiv

Mathematical summary.

There is a ring equivalence between the rational chart 𝒪_𝓐({|W| ≤ |ϖ|}) and K⟨X,Q⟩/(Q²). The forward map applies the quotient 𝓐 → 𝓑, which discards the terms divisible by Q², and then substitutes W=ϖX; the completed chart kills Q²𝓒. The inverse evaluates K⟨X⟩ at W/ϖ and adjoins the square-zero image of Q. The inverse identities use the decomposition into the terms of Q-degree at most one and the part in Q²𝓒, together with polynomial density. The companion continuity theorems make this a homeomorphism.
