FiniteJet.GraphKoszul.koszulDifferential_isStrict

Mathematical summary.

For every q ≥ 0, the Koszul differential d_{q+1,E}: ∧^{q+1}T_E^m → ∧^qT_E^m is strict onto its image.

In the Gauss-norm/unit-ball case formalised in Lean, this follows from norm-controlled lifts from the image.
