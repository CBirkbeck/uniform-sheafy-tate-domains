FiniteJet.GraphKoszul.koszulGraph_exact_strict_closed

Mathematical summary.

Let E be a complete ultrametric normed commutative ring with ‖1‖=1. Assume that the norm unit ball E°, the restricted power-series ring T_E=E⟨T₁,…,T_m⟩, and the norm unit ball of T_E are noetherian. Suppose that t∈E is a unit with 0<‖t‖<1 and ‖tx‖=‖t‖‖x‖ for every x. If g,f₁,…,f_m generate the unit ideal and r_i=gT_i-f_i, then the Koszul complex on (r_i) is exact in every positive degree, every differential is strict onto its image, and every image is closed. This is the full generality of the exactness, strictness, and closed-image part of Lemma 5.1 formalised in Lean.

theorem koszulGraph_exact_strict_closed [IsNoetherianRing (P E m)]
    (hE₀ : IsNoetherianRing (unitBall E))
    (t : E) (htu : IsUnit t) (ht1 : ‖t‖ < 1) (ht0 : 0 < ‖t‖)
    (hscale : ∀ x : E, ‖t * x‖ = ‖t‖ * ‖x‖)
    (hE₀P : IsNoetherianRing (unitBall (P E m)))
    (g : E) (f : Fin m → E) (hunit : Ideal.span ({g} ∪ Set.range f) = ⊤)
    (r : Fin m → P E m)
    (hr : ∀ i, r i = polyToP (MvPolynomial.C g * MvPolynomial.X i - MvPolynomial.C (f i))) :
    (∀ q, Function.Exact (koszulDifferential r (q + 1)) (koszulDifferential r q)) ∧
      (∀ q, IsStrictLinearMap (koszulDifferential r q)) ∧
      (∀ q, IsClosed (Set.range (koszulDifferential r q)))
