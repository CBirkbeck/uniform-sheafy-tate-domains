FiniteJet.Pinch.isClosed_IA

Mathematical summary.

Under the same abstract pinch-square and noetherian hypotheses, the graph ideal I_A=(gT_i-f_i)_i is closed in T_A. Lean combines closedness of I_B and I_C with the bounded ideal-pullback theorem.
