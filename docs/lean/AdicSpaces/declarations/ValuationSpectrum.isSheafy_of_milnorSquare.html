ValuationSpectrum.isSheafy_of_milnorSquare

Mathematical summary.

Let R,B,C,D be complete nonarchimedean Huber rings. Suppose that every rational datum U on R can be pushed functorially to data U_B,U_C,U_D, that pushed rational covers are still covers, and that for every U the map 𝒪_R(U) → 𝒪_B(U_B)×𝒪_C(U_C) is a topological embedding whose image is exactly the equalizer of the two maps to 𝒪_D(U_D). Assume also that these maps commute with restriction and preserve compatibility of matching families. If B, C, and D satisfy IsSheafy, then so does R.

The formal theorem uses precisely the equalizer and topological-embedding properties needed for descent; it does not separately require the map 𝒪_B(U_B)⊕𝒪_C(U_C)→𝒪_D(U_D) to be surjective.
