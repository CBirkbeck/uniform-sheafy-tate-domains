FiniteJet.ev00

Mathematical summary.

For the finite-jet algebra over K=F((t)), ev00 : 𝒜 → K sends f₀(W)+Qf₁(W)+Q²h to f₀(0). It is the composite of the map to the square-zero disc algebra, projection to its degree-zero part, and evaluation at W=0. The companion norm estimate shows that it is continuous and carries the norm unit ball into the valuation ring; pulling back the norm valuation produces the point of the chart used in Proposition 7.1.

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

The origin evaluation ev₀₀ : 𝓐 → K, f₀(W) + Qf₁(W) + Q²h ↦ f₀(0): discard the
terms of Q-degree ≥ 1 (the map to 𝓑), then evaluate the disc component at W = 0.
