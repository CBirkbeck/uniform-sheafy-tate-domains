FiniteJet.canonicalMap_scottishWitness

Mathematical summary.

For a base satisfying IsFJPBase K and the rational datum (W;ϖ), the canonical map from the finite-jet algebra to the completed rational localisation sends Q² to zero. This is the completion phenomenon calculated in Proposition 4.1: the corresponding identity ρ(Q)²=0 was proved before identifying the completed chart with K⟨X,Q⟩/(Q²).

The mathematical summary is editorial text for this paper. The declaration below is extracted verbatim from the pinned source.

Original Lean docstring

Q² dies in the completed rational localization — [FJP] (3.3), through the
already-proved canonicalMap_Qa_sq. This is a completion phenomenon: ϖ is a unit of
𝓐, so the *algebraic* localization at ϖ is 𝓐 itself, where Q² ≠ 0; the element
becomes zero only after imposing that W/ϖ be power-bounded and completing.
