ValuationSpectrum.isSheafyComplete_of_stronglyNoetherianTate

Mathematical summary.

Every complete separated nonarchimedean strongly noetherian Tate ring is sheafy for every ring of integral elements. This is the complete ring-level form of Wedhorn’s strongly-noetherian sheafiness theorem used for the noetherian vertices in the paper.

Original Lean docstring

The principal ring-level theorem (Wedhorn Theorem 8.28(b), complete case): a
complete strongly noetherian Tate ring satisfies the universal (Definition 8.26)
sheafiness — *every* valid ring of integral elements gives a sheafy pair, with the
genuine structure presheaf and projective-limit topology.

Used in the paper for: Appendix A (complete strongly noetherian Tate ring, every chosen pair)
