FiniteJet.Pinch.ideal_pullback_controlled

Mathematical summary.

For an abstract pinch square satisfying the noetherian vertex hypotheses, every compatible pair (x_B,x_C)∈I_B×_{I_D}I_C comes from an element x_A∈I_A, with ‖x_A‖≤C max(‖x_B‖,‖x_C‖) for one fixed constant C. The proof uses bounded lifts for d₁, corrects the resulting discrepancy by a bounded d₂-lift over D, and pulls the corrected coefficients back through the original square.

theorem ideal_pullback_controlled (hN : NoethPack B C D m)
    (hspan : Ideal.span ({g} ∪ Set.range f) = ⊤) :
    ∃ Cs : ℝ, 1 ≤ Cs ∧
      ∀ xb ∈ S.IB m g f, ∀ xc ∈ S.IC m g f,
        S.extDB m xb = S.extDC m xc →
        ∃ xa ∈ IA m g f,
          S.extB m xa = xb ∧ S.extC m xa = xc ∧ ‖xa‖ ≤ Cs * max ‖xb‖ ‖xc‖
