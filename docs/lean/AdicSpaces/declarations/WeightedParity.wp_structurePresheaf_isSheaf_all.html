WeightedParity.wp_structurePresheaf_isSheaf_all

Mathematical summary.

For any valid ring of integral elements A⁺, the all-open structure presheaf on Spa(𝒜,A⁺) is a sheaf. This is the direct all-open form of the weighted-parity sheaf theorem.
