FiniteJetOver.isSheafy_JetA

Mathematical summary.

Choose a uniformizer of K and suppose that the unit ball of K is noetherian. Then the maximal pair (𝓐,𝓐°) satisfies the finite rational-cover condition IsSheafy. Lean pushes a cover and matching family to the sheafy vertices 𝓑,𝓒,𝓓, glues over 𝓑 and 𝓒, uses separation over 𝓓, and pulls the result back through the localized Milnor row. The restriction map has the closed matching equalizer as its range, so a closed-range theorem makes it a topological embedding.
