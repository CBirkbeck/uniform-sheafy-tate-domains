WeightedParity.evenSupportEquiv

Mathematical summary.

Fix any weight w:ℕ→ℕ. Let 𝒜_w⁽ᴺ⁾ be the subring using only W,U₁,…,U_N, and let 𝒯_w⁽ᴺ⁾ be its subring in which every U_i has even exponent. Halving those exponents gives a ring equivalence 𝒯_w⁽ᴺ⁾ ≃ K⟨W,Z₁,…,Z_N⟩; its inverse substitutes Z_i ↦ U_i². No positivity or unboundedness assumption on w is needed.
