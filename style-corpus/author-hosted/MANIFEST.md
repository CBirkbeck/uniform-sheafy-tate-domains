# Author-hosted style source

- Christopher Birkbeck, *Formalising modular forms, Eisenstein series and the
  statement of the modularity conjecture* (2023), author-hosted PDF:
  <https://e79948fa-9217-4b51-a99b-1ead710a701c.filesusr.com/ugd/2ba265_f8f43aec255349ac92b639c877e0c6cf.pdf>.

This is the only paper listed on the author's publication page that is not in
the arXiv corpus.  It is especially relevant to the presentation of Lean code:
the mathematical definition is followed by a short declaration and an
explanation of how the formal fields recover the usual notion.
